# Other Research Notes

## Working Thesis

AI-powered SEO content production is not just "publish more articles with AI." The stronger pattern from the source set is:

1. Start with product, ICP, buying journey, and bottom-funnel questions.
2. Use AI for research acceleration, clustering, outline testing, refresh analysis, and production assistance.
3. Add differentiated inputs: SME interviews, customer language, product data, internal benchmarks, examples, demos, and opinion.
4. Structure content so both humans and AI systems can retrieve precise passages.
5. Distribute content into places that create third-party validation: LinkedIn, YouTube, podcasts, Reddit, partner sites, review pages, and comparison pages.
6. Measure qualified demand, citations, mentions, AI visibility, pipeline, and content refresh lift, not only traffic.
